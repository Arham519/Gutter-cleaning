# FAQs

**How much does gutter cleaning cost in Palm Coast?**
Most single story homes pay between about $120 and $250 per visit, based on national pricing data from sources like Angi and HomeGuide. Two story homes usually cost more because of the extra height and equipment needed. The exact price depends on your gutter length and debris buildup, so we always confirm a firm number before starting any work.

**How often should gutters be cleaned in Florida?**
Most homes need cleaning at least twice a year, in spring and fall. Homes surrounded by oak or pine trees often need three to four cleanings a year, since needles and leaves build up faster. If your home sits near heavy tree cover, it's worth checking more often than the average schedule.

**When's the best time to clean gutters before hurricane season?**
Late spring, just before hurricane season starts in June, is one of the best times to clean your gutters. This clears spring leaf drop before the heaviest rain arrives. A second cleaning in early fall helps catch storm debris left over from summer weather, and cleaning again after any major storm is a good habit too.

**Are gutter guards worth it?**
Gutter guards can cut down on how often you need a cleaning, but they don't remove the need for maintenance completely. Fine pine needles and pollen still settle on top of most guards and should be cleared at least once a year. Guards work best as a way to reduce cleanings, not eliminate them.

**Is it safe to clean your own gutters?**
DIY gutter cleaning can work for a single story home with easy ladder access and light debris. It carries real risk on a two story home, a steep roof, or with heavy buildup, since ladder falls are a common cause of home injury. Beyond a simple single story job, professional cleaning is the safer choice.
