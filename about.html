Frequently Asked Questions

Professional gutter cleaning includes removing leaves, roof grit, seed pods, and other debris that restrict water flow. The service also includes clearing visible blockages, checking the roof drainage system, and making sure rainwater can move away from your home properly. Before the visit is complete, any visible drainage concerns are explained.
The cost depends on several factors, including the size of your home, roof height, accessibility, and the amount of debris inside the system. Every property is different, so an inspection is the best way to receive an accurate estimate.
Most Palm Coast homes benefit from maintenance at least twice a year. Homes surrounded by mature trees or exposed to frequent storms may require more frequent service because debris can build up faster throughout the year.
For many homeowners, gutter guards help reduce the amount of leaves and outdoor debris that enter the drainage system. They make routine maintenance easier, but they do not eliminate the need for periodic inspections and cleaning.
Heavy rainfall can move large amounts of water through the roof drainage system in a short period. If debris blocks the flow, water may overflow instead of draining away from the home. Routine maintenance before the rainy season helps reduce the chance of drainage problems.
